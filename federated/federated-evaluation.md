Evaluation is a **first-class systems problem** in FL: centralised test assumptions often fail when data and outcome definitions remain distributed. A substantial literature now treats federated evaluation in its own right.

Relates to: [Evaluation rubric](evaluation-rubric.md), [Non-IID data](non-iid-data.md).
