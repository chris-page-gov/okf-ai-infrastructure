Data across clients is not independently and identically distributed; heterogeneity is a **structural condition**, not noise. Any architecture assuming IID data or uniform device availability will underperform in realistic settings, motivating clustering, personalisation and heterogeneity-aware methods.

Relates to: [Federated learning](federated-learning.md), [Kairouz: advances and open problems](../research/kairouz-open-problems.md).
